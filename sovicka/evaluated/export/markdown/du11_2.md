<summary>Evaluator justification (English)</summary>

Direct multiplication gives AB=I_3, ABA=A, and BAB=B, but BA is not symmetric. The written claim that BA=I_4 is impossible because rank(BA)≤rank(A)=3 and is contradicted by the displayed product. A correct construction with a decisive arithmetic/verification failure merits 3/4.</p>
